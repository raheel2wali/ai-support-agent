# Store FAQs

## Return policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 working days. To start a return, just ask here and we'll open a ticket for you.

## Shipping info

We ship nationwide across Pakistan. Orders placed before 2pm ship the same day. Delivery takes 3-5 working days for most cities including Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad. Shipping is free on orders over Rs. 2,000, otherwise Rs. 200.

## Tracking your package

Every shipped order gets a tracking number by SMS and email. You can also ask me "where is my order <id>?" and I'll look it up directly.

## Payment methods

We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, and all major debit/credit cards. Card payments are processed securely — we never see or store your full card number.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or something's missing, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and we'll sort it out — replacement or refund, your choice.

## Contacting support

Our support team works 9am–9pm PKT, 7 days a week. Anything I can't handle gets flagged to them automatically.
